# CCMS Image Guide

This directory is structured to support the art direction and future photography rollout for Cuddles Childminders & Schools.

## Directory map
- branding/: logo, marks, institutional brand moments
- campus/: school grounds and environmental photography
- students/: children, groups, candid learning moments
- classroom/: literacy, numeracy, classroom scenes
- coding/: tech, robotics, coding activities
- art/: exhibitions, creative work, student projects
- innovation/: competitions, business ideas, innovation showcases
- achievements/: award and competition photography
- events/: open days, exhibitions, special gatherings
- gallery/: editorial portfolio images
- leadership/: staff, management, guidance moments
- admissions/: onboarding, tours, prospective family moments

## Naming conventions
- Use lowercase, hyphenated filenames.
- Prefer descriptive names such as `classroom-reading-session.jpg`.
- Keep a consistent aspect ratio for each section.

## Recommended dimensions
- Hero image: 1600 x 1800 portrait or 1800 x 1200 landscape
- Section imagery: 1200 x 900 to 1600 x 1100
- Portraits: 900 x 1200
- Gallery: 900 x 700

## Placeholder status
- Current placeholders are based on remote assets and should be replaced with local CCMS photography when available.
- Replace these placeholders in `src/data/images.ts` when final school photography is supplied.

## Replacement instructions
1. Add the final image to the appropriate folder.
2. Update the corresponding record in `src/data/images.ts`.
3. Confirm crop and aspect ratio suit the target layout.
4. Keep alt text descriptive and human-centred.
